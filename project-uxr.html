Rome2Rio · 2025–2026

Building a UXR practice

Rome2Rio was building the product without a strong understanding of what users need. I set up a continuous research programme with fortnightly interviews, a shared insight repository and findings built into planning, so research shapes strategy before it's set instead of after.

Research operations Strategic research Cross-functional collaboration

My role

Product Design Lead

Stakeholders

Product, engineering, brand and Omio legal

Outcome

Research as habit, not event

The problem

Product decisions were being made without a steady picture of how users think and behave. Research happened on its own, usually testing a feature that was already designed or checking a hunch. The team couldn't easily find what we'd learned either, and nothing turned that understanding into an advantage.

The core principle

Research should shape strategy instead of just checking features. When there's nothing specific to test there is still plenty to learn, and open-ended qualitative research is where most of the strategic value is.

What I built: three phases

1
Foundations (H1 2026) Get access to participants through HotJar intercepts and our existing user pools. Keep the rhythm light with a 30-minute exploratory interview every fortnight and no prototype needed. Build a searchable research repo on Confluence with one page per study covering findings and implications.
2
Informing strategy (Q3 2026) Link research to strategic questions by listing the user behaviour assumptions that need to be true for each major bet. Run a foundational mental models study. Share findings in rituals we already have like sprint planning and design crits as well as a Slack channel for quick insights.
3
Building for scale (Q4 2026+) Add more methods over time, like diary studies for behaviour over several days, unmoderated usability tests for quick iteration and surveys to size the themes. Go from monthly to continuous as capacity grows.

"Make research a habit instead of an event. Start small and build trust."

How it works

Every two weeks we run a 30-minute exploratory interview with someone from our panel. The topics shift with what the team is thinking about, like where people lose confidence in a trip, how they think about multi-stop routes or how they move between devices. There's no prototype and no script, just a conversation.

Findings go into a shared Confluence repo that the whole team can open. Each study records the question, the method, the key findings and what they mean for us. Engineers can pull the details with AI tools. Product leads see what users said in context and designers use real quotes to back up decisions in crit.

A Slack channel shares standout observations each month. Quarterly planning has a what we heard slot so findings can reduce the risk in strategic bets before they're committed.

Making it outlast me

I got the go-ahead from Omio legal to recruit users globally then set up the panel and wrote templates so picking up a study doesn't start from a blank page. Findings sit in rituals the team already has like sprint planning, crit and a Slack channel, so research carries on without me in the room.

Results

50+ Participants recruited into the panel across our target markets
→ A steady stream of insight for product strategy and design decisions
✓ A shared Research and Insights repo used by Rome2Rio and Omio teams
