LoyaltyCorp · 2016–2019

eWallet

LoyaltyCorp's eWallet gives people one place for their purchases, points, bank accounts and funds inside any loyalty program, so brand loyalty and payments sit together. It is embedded across all LoyaltyCorp platforms. I led the end-to-end design of the consumer widget and the payment flows.

Fintech White-label Payments 2FA

My role

Lead product designer

Team

Engineering, PM, client stakeholders

Scope

Widget, payments, auth flows

The challenge

Loyalty program members had no single place to manage their rewards, payments and account details. Every task meant switching between separate systems with different interfaces, which caused friction right where trust mattered most. LoyaltyCorp needed a white-label solution that clients could adapt, that could sit inside any loyalty platform and that could handle real financial transactions securely.

What I designed

1
White-labelled consumer widget I designed a fully adaptable widget that client brands could style to match their identity. It supports theming at token level so each deployment felt native to the client's platform with no redesign work.
2
Secure card and bank detail storage I designed the flows for adding, verifying and managing saved payment methods, including card verification with a small test amount as well as secure storage screens that show people their financial data is safe.
3
2FA and verification flows I designed two-factor authentication for card verification and account security, balancing compliance with low friction. It had to work reliably on different networks and devices.
4
End-to-end payment flows I designed the full payment journey covering P2P transfers, recurring payments, instalments and bill pay. Every flow shares one interaction pattern while allowing for the confirmation and error states each payment type needs.
5
Client feature adaptability I worked with the product team on a feature flag system at the UI level so each client could switch payment features on or off without the widget looking inconsistent.
"Bridging the gap between brand loyalty and seamless transacting."

Key design decisions

Financial products depend on trust and trust is fragile in white-label settings where people may not recognise the brand behind the interface. Every interaction was designed to feel deliberate and in control, with no unclear states, a clear confirmation at every step and error messages that say exactly what happened and what to do next.

I treated the white-label constraint as a strength. With the widget built on a proper token system each client could bring their own brand without changing the interaction patterns people had already learned, which kept things consistent where it mattered most.

Outcomes

5+

Client deployments supported by the white-label system

4 flows

Payment types designed, covering P2P, recurring, instalment and bill pay

1 widget

Single design system powering all client deployments
